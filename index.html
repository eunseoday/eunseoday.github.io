Eunseo Jeong

AI / Computer Vision Researcher

Yonsei University


I received my M.S. in Computer Science from Yonsei University, where I conducted research at the High-Performance HCI Lab, advised by Prof. Byungjoo Lee. My research focused on understanding human behavior through multimodal data, including motion capture, sEMG, video, and interaction logs, and modeling human motor behavior using reinforcement learning in physics-based simulations.

My primary research interests lie in 3D computer vision and reinforcement learning, with a focus on understanding and modeling human movement and interaction.
